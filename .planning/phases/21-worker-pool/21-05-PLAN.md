<objective>
The [Step limit] of 21-CONTEXT.md: a global limit on the number of save steps that run at once (flusher steps plus writers that pay with their own file), so peak memory does not grow with the number of writers or workers. Plan 21-04 gave the S7 knob and the numbers before the limit; plan 21-06 measures the limit.

Decisions that this plan implements (labels of 21-CONTEXT.md):
- [Step limit] (user answer, 2026-11-23): "Keep 200 ms. This phase adds a global limit on the number of save steps that run at once (flusher steps plus writers that pay with their own file) ... A writer that cannot get a step slot waits with no lock held (time limit, ends at stop), or writes without a step only if the per-file hard cap still holds; the planner picks the exact rule." The rule is in the context below; it is reversible internal design. The orchestrator reading of the user answer (21-CONTEXT.md): 320 MiB is a hard bar at 1, 2, 4 and 8 writers; plan 21-06 judges it.
- [Throttle]: the 200 ms throttle limit stays; its effect on S7 (VmHWM 262-340 MiB in 7 runs, one over CAP_MIB 320) is the reason for the limit (runs/s7-throttle-200ms.log).
- [Waits]: every new wait has a time limit and ends at the flusher stop (the stop runs at unmount); no wait while a ranked lock is held.
- [Stats] (orchestrator, 2026-11-23): the step limit keeps cheap atomic counters next to the Phase 20 flusher stats (slot waits and their time, save steps and steps per second, bytes freed per step), readable by tests and printed at unmount. No controller (the adaptive control is a deferred idea).

Purpose: the memory bound does not depend on the worker count.
Output: the slot rule with its tests, the release admission, the S7 slot fields, a source guard.

The step-slot rule (planner decision for [Step limit]; reversible):
- `SAVE_STEPS = 2`: while the flusher runs, at most 2 overlay save steps (calls of `rebase_unlocked` that run their I/O) run at once in one file system, counting flusher steps and request steps together. Reason: with the 5 s limit, about one step (the flusher's) ran at a time and VmHWM stayed at or under 289 MiB; with 200 ms up to 5 ran at once (the flusher and 4 paying writers) and one run reached 340 MiB. Test hook `test_set_save_steps(n)`.
- `SLOT_WAIT = 200 ms`, the per-call pause cap of `THROTTLE_WAIT`; a write over the budget waits at most 200 ms in the throttle plus 200 ms for a slot. Test hook `test_set_slot_wait(ms)`. The 150 s pool limits of 21-01 already count it (130.4 s per request).
- The slot count lives in `FlushCtl`, under the `flags` lock (no new ranked lock), with a new condvar for "a slot is free". A `StepSlot` guard (it holds an `Arc<FlushCtl>`) frees its slot and notifies on drop. While a thread holds a `StepSlot`, a thread-local count says so.
- A blocking take, `pub fn take_step_slot(&self) -> SlotTake`, with `pub enum SlotTake { Got(StepSlot), NotNeeded, TimedOut, Stopped }`: debug-asserts an empty rank stack (as `throttle_write`); gives `NotNeeded` at once when no flusher loop runs (`ctl.running` false: replay, in-process callers, and the many flusher tests that call `flush_pass` directly with no loop); else waits in `THROTTLE_SLICE` slices until a slot is free (`Got`), the flusher stops (`Stopped`), or `SLOT_WAIT` passed (`TimedOut`, counted in `slot_timeouts`). Counted in `slot_waits` when it had to wait. Four outcomes, because the callers must tell them apart (below).
- A non-blocking take, `fn try_step_slot(&self) -> Option<StepSlot>`: no wait; it may run with the gate and a cell held, because it takes only the `Rank::Short` flags lock.
- No flusher running (replay, in-process callers with no flusher, `test_destroy` after the stop): no slot is needed, every step runs as today.
- Write admission (in `throttle_write`, before the gate): when its answer is `pay: true` and the flusher runs, it calls `take_step_slot`. `Got`: `Admit` carries the slot for the whole write (it is dropped when `handle_write_admitted` returns). `TimedOut`: the answer becomes `pay: false` (the write goes in with no budget step; the hard-cap gate of `write_in_cell` still holds), counted in its own counter `pay_skips`. `Stopped` or `NotNeeded`: `pay: true` with no slot (Phase 20 rule; no limit then). `Admit` stops being `Copy`; add constructors for the two literals (for example `Admit::pay_now()`).
- Release admission, `pub fn admit_release(&self, fh: u64) -> Option<StepSlot>` (before the gate): when `fh` is a write handle and its cell has a dirty cost above 0 (atomic reads after short map lookups, no wait under them), it calls `take_step_slot`; on `Got` the caller keeps the slot until the release returns; on any other outcome the release goes on with no slot.
- Flusher (`flush_step`, before its gate hold): `take_step_slot`. `Got`: step with the slot. `NotNeeded`: step as today (a direct `flush_pass` call in a test with no loop). `TimedOut` or `Stopped`: a new `FlushStep::NoSlot`; `flush_pass` then ends the pass at once and does not change `pass_stuck` or `empty_pass` (request threads hold the slots and free memory).
- Inside `rebase_unlocked(g, free, need)`, a new argument `need: Need` (`Need::May` or `Need::Must`): when the thread holds a slot, or no flusher runs, the step runs. Else `try_step_slot`: a slot, the step runs and frees it at its end. No slot: `Need::May` returns `Ok(false)` (skipped, counted in `slot_skips`); `Need::Must` runs with no slot (counted in `slot_overruns`).
- `Need::May`: `rebase_cell` (the per-file-limit step of a writer, and through `free_dirty` the budget step and the flusher step; the file stays under its hard cap). `Need::Must`: `cap_step` (both sites; the hard cap is the per-file bound) and `finalize_steps` (a release must store its file).
- Not counted (no copy of overlay data): stream pushes, gap fills, the switch to an overlay, the stream flush, `empty_rule`, and the locked `rebase_step` of `test_destroy`.
- Stats (`FlusherStats`): `slot_waits`, `slot_timeouts` (all blocking takes that timed out: writes, releases, the flusher), `pay_skips` (write admissions that timed out and skipped the budget step), `slot_skips`, `slot_overruns`, and `max_save_steps` (the high-water mark of steps in flight, overruns included). Per [Stats], also: `slot_wait_ms_total` and `slot_wait_ms_max` (the time of the blocking takes that waited), `save_steps` (save steps that ran to the end) and `freed_bytes` (the dirty cost they freed, so bytes per step = `freed_bytes / save_steps`). All are `AtomicU64` in `FlushCtl`, updated with `Relaxed` adds where the step or the wait ends. At unmount, `MountedFs::destroy` prints one line after `shutdown()`: `steps: save_steps=<n> steps_per_s=<x> freed_per_step=<bytes> slot_waits=<n> slot_wait_ms_total=<ms> slot_wait_ms_max=<ms> slot_timeouts=<n> pay_skips=<n> slot_skips=<n> slot_overruns=<n> max_save_steps=<n>`, where steps per second is `save_steps` over the time since the flusher started.
- Deadlock check (state it in the doc of `take_step_slot`): a slot holder never waits for a second slot (one blocking take per request, before the gate; tries inside); the flusher holds at most one; a blocking take holds no lock; a slot holder may wait for the gate behind a queued commit, and the commit waits only for current shared holders, which never wait for a slot. A slot holder may wait for the step token of its own cell (bounded by `STEP_WAIT_MAX`), held by another slot holder or an overrun step, which needs no slot to end.
- New dirty-total bound: a write whose slot wait timed out adds at most one write (`len + EXTENT_OVERHEAD`) above the Phase 20 bound, and each file stays under its hard cap. So with W writers: total <= budget + W x (write + EXTENT_OVERHEAD) + SAVE_STEPS x REBASE_STEP + pay_skips x (write + EXTENT_OVERHEAD). Use `pay_skips`, not `slot_timeouts`: flusher and release timeouts add no write.
