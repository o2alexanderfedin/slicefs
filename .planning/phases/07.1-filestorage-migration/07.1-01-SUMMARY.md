## Decisions Made

- Used `S: StorageAdd + blockset::storage::StorageGet` bound for mixed read/write directory functions instead of splitting into two parameters. Split params would require `(&mut storage, &storage)` which Rust's borrow checker disallows on the same value. The combined bound works because `Dictionary` implements both, and callers pass a single `&mut Dictionary`.
- `load_entry_list` and `list_dir_entries` generalized to `&impl StorageGet` (not just `&Dictionary`) — this makes them forward-compatible with file-backed readers in Plan 02/03.
