<objective>
Finish LOCK-04: append every namespace record under the stripes (and cell) of its apply, replay creates by the logged inode number, and prove with the mixed-operation harness that replay after a crash equals the apply.

Decision IDs are numbered in 19-01-PLAN.md (objective). This plan implements:
- D-08 (namespace path and replay) the log record append (memory copy plus LSN) happens under the same locks as its apply; disk write and sync happen outside the locks; replay creates an inode with the logged inode number.
- D-04 (use) the stripes from Plan 09 are the apply locks of namespace records.

RESEARCH: Pattern 3 (log under the apply locks; write-out after every apply lock is dropped, before the reply), Pitfall 9 (creates appended in reverse inode order: hand-built records test), Risk 6 (the log callback is a no-op during replay), Inventory C dependency 8, Wave 0 gap `tests/common/mod.rs`. The crash-replay check runs on the Phase 18 harness (ROADMAP criterion 5 says "from the mixed-operation harness"), in `stress_harness.rs`.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 5.
Output: namespace logging under locks in `handlers.rs`/`filesystem.rs`, replay by inode in `oplog.rs`, shared test helpers, `crash_replay_matches_apply`.
</objective>

- After Plan 09 every namespace function holds its stripes (and the inode's cell for setattr/xattr) through `lock_namespace` / `NsGuard`.
