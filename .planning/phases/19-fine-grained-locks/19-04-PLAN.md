<objective>
Split the write log into an in-memory append (LSN ticket, a memory copy under a short queue lock) and a write-out after the request's apply locks, and move every WAL fsync out of the WAL mutexes. This is the log side of LOCK-04: Plans 08 and 10 call `log_append` inside the apply locks.

Decision IDs are numbered in 19-01-PLAN.md (objective). This plan implements:
- D-08 (log side) The log record append (memory copy plus LSN) happens under the same locks as its apply; disk write and sync happen outside the locks. Here: the API that makes this possible (`append` is memory only; `write_out` and the sync hold no lock the apply needs).
- D-06 (use): all WAL locks are `Rank::Store`; the new queue lock is timed as `Site::WalQueue`.
Leader/follower group commit and `durable_lsn` are not in this phase (CONTEXT boundary: WAL group commit is deferred).

RESEARCH Lock Inventory A rows 9-11 and Pitfall 6 (lock order inversion in the split) are closed here.

Purpose: LOCK-01 names the WAL append mutex; Phase 18 measured its hold at p50 1.2-2.4 ms with a 21 ms max (an fsync).
Output: the WAL strategies, `SegmentWriter` sync split, store log functions, the `wal_queue` lock-stats site.
</objective>

Facts (verified at 2c39d65; grep the names, lines moved in Plans 01 and 03):
- `trait WalStrategy` (`wal/mod.rs` near 79): `log_mutation`, `flush_and_sync`, `shutdown`, `rotate(seed)`, `cut() -> Option<WalCut>`, `install_root(cut, seed)`. Implementations: `PerOpWal` (`per_op.rs`: `writer`, `loc`, `behind` mutexes), `BufferedLog` (`buffered.rs`: `pending`, `writer`, `loc`, `behind`; comment "Lock order everywhere: pending, then writer"), `FlushOnFsyncWal`, `PeriodicWal` (a timer thread calls `flush_and_sync`), `NoWal`.
- `PerOpWal::log_mutation` writes and flushes the record under `writer`; `flush_and_sync` holds `writer` across `sync()` and calls `behind.settle()` (fsyncs) under `behind`. `install_root` already syncs a cloned file (`SegmentWriter::file_for_sync`) with no lock held.
- `SegmentWriter` (`segment/writer.rs`) has `unsynced: bool`, `disk_syncs: u64`, `sync()`, `file_for_sync()`.
- `BUFFER_CAP_BYTES` = 4 MiB (`wal/mod.rs:37`): buffered strategies hold records up to it and write them out past it.
- `DictMetadataStore::log_op(bytes)` -> `log_wal_entry(&WalEntry::Op { bytes })`, which locks the store's `wal` slot (`Site::Wal`) across `log_mutation`; a failed log bumps `wal_fail_seq`; `flush_wal` fails while `wal_fail_seq` differs from `wal_fail_cleared`; `wal_handle()` clones the strategy `Arc`. `cut()` runs under the exclusive commit gate.
- `slicefs-cli` calls `meta.log_op` from `filesystem.rs` `log_record` (handlers' `Logged::log` and the commit relog); that call site does not change here.
