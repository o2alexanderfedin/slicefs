**dedup_index.rs** — `DedupResult` enum with `DefinitelyAbsent`, `Present`, `Absent`. Define `DedupIndex` trait with `bloom_check`, `lookup`, `insert`, `remove` methods per the research. Trait must be `Send + Sync`.

**lib.rs** — Declare `pub mod` for all modules. Re-export all public types and traits via `pub use` for ergonomic imports.

Important: All traits use `&self` (not `&mut self`) for thread safety with `RwLock`/`Mutex` in implementations. Follow the research code examples closely — they have been vetted.
