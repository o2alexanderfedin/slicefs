The lock_tail failure: `lock_tail_tests::put_and_read_pass_a_held_keep_cap_wait` panicked with `timed out: A waits for the background sync at KEEP_CAP` (lock_tail_tests.rs:41), after 9.93 s (macOS) and 8.10 s (Linux). It is deterministic in a debug TSan build (2 of 2 reruns alone). The test puts KEEP_CAP / 1 MiB + 2 = 26 objects of 1 MiB with a 5 s real-time limit per step; it takes 0.79 s in a plain debug build, 0.07 s in release, and 0.53 s under TSan in release, with no report. So it is the step time limit under the sanitizer, not a hang and not a race. Miri ignores the same test for the same reason (plan 05). Rule applied (reversible, plan rule 8): keep the module, skip that one test through `TSAN_LIB_SKIP`, with the reason above the name in tsan.sh. The other 11 tests of the module stay under TSan.
