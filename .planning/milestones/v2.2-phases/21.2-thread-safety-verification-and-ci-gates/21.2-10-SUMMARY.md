## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 3] Own image tag for the local runs.** The local runs use `slicefs-fusetest-cov`, not `slicefs-fusetest`: other worktrees rebuild `slicefs-fusetest` from their own Dockerfile, without the coverage tools. run.sh (the CI path) still builds `slicefs-fusetest`. One `run.sh` usage check and the job-command run rebuilt `slicefs-fusetest` with this Dockerfile; the change only adds tools.
2. **[Rule 3] No `slicefs-rustup-p21` volume.** Mounted over `/usr/local/rustup` it would hide the `llvm-tools-preview` of the image. The image toolchain is 1.100.0, the same as the pin.
3. **`-j 2` and volume `slicefs-target-p21-cov`** instead of the plan's `-j 1` and `slicefs-target-p21` (orchestrator rule).
4. **Coverage build in `<target>/cov`** (not in the plan). `show-env` gives the instrumentation through `RUSTC_WRAPPER`, and `cargo llvm-cov clean --workspace` removes workspace artifacts; a subdirectory keeps the plain builds of the other run.sh modes on the same volume safe.
5. **Smoke profiles in a side directory** (not in the plan): without it, a report-only run would count the smoke profiles in the gated tests number.
6. Extra log lines (not `COVERAGE` lines): `coverage: <tool versions>`, `coverage: smoke profiles=<n>`, and the `TOTAL` row of the gate report.
