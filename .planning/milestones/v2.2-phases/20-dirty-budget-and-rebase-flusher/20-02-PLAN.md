<objective>
Tracer of Phase 20: one path through every layer that the phase changes. A writer over the shared dirty budget sleeps with no lock held; a flusher thread frees memory with steps of other files; the writer then goes on and does not pay. After the wait limit the writer pays with steps of its own file.

Decisions that this plan implements (labels of 20-CONTEXT.md):
- [When dirty data is written] (user): trigger 3, "the buffer is full: the shared dirty total is at or above the background threshold (half of `--dirty-budget`) ... The flusher then steps the largest dirty file first"; and "A writer over the budget sleeps (with no lock held) until the flusher frees memory. After the wait limit (default 5 s), the writer does not fail: it writes, and pays for it by one unlocked step of its own file (the Phase 19 path). No new error for applications."
- [Flusher] threads: one flusher; it takes the commit gate shared for each step, never across a sleep, and steps one file at a time under the Phase 19 step token.
- [Throttle]: the writer waits in the write entry, before the gate, the stripes and the cell, on a condvar that the flusher signals after each step, in slices, up to the wait limit.
- [Waits]: every new wait has a time limit.
- [Harness]: the default harness `run()` does not start the flusher.

Out of this plan: the hot rule and the idle and expire triggers (plan 20-05; this plan's pick is "largest first", which plan 20-05 narrows by A10), the mount wiring, stop at unmount and the panic guard (plan 20-03), the deadlock tests (plan 20-04).

Purpose: prove the admission, the flusher step and the pay path end to end before the policy and the lifecycle are built on them.
Output: `flusher.rs`, the throttle, the pay flag, `handle_write_admitted`, five tests in `tests/flusher_tests.rs`.
</objective>
