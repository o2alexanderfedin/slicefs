<objective>
Lifecycle of the flusher: start it with the mount, wire the throttle into the FUSE `write` callback, and make every wait end at unmount or at a flusher panic. Make the destroy order callable in process and test it with a parked flusher step and a queued commit.

Decisions that this plan implements (labels of 20-CONTEXT.md):
- [Waits]: "All new waits have a time limit and exit when the mount shuts down (unmount wakes the flusher and the throttled writers)."
- [Flusher] threads: "One thread, spawned with the mount like the commit timer".
- [Throttle]: the wait is in the write entry, before the commit gate, the stripes and the cell.
- [Deadlock]: "A commit never waits for budget."
- Research Q2 "Stop in destroy, before the gate": a join under the gate can deadlock with a queued commit.

Purpose: the daemon can stop at any time, and no writer or commit waits for a flusher that is gone.
Output: `spawn_flusher`, `start_flusher`, `stop_flusher` with the wake and the join, `shutdown`, the FUSE wiring, the mount wiring, five tests and two unit tests, one Docker smoke mount.
</objective>
