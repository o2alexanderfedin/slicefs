Find each place by its heading, not by a line number.

What the recover step measures now: it moves .idx files away and opens. Packs below the high-water mark are served by the runs, so their missing .idx is not read and not rebuilt; only packs at or above the mark take the scan path. The run driver does not fail on this: when the extra time is not positive it prints n/a for the scan rates (step_run). But scanned_bytes and scanned_objects would still count every dropped pack, which is wrong, so Task 1 counts only the packs that the open really scanned. The Recovery rows of the new index are about the same as a normal open; say this in the doc, and do not compare them with the baseline recovery rows as like for like.
